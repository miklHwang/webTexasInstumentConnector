How to Connect

Four steps, no installer.

  1. Use Chrome or EdgeOn Windows, macOS, Linux, ChromeOS or Android. Firefox and Safari don't support the USB features this needs.
  2. Plug in your calculatorUse its USB cable. Calculators with only a round link port need a SilverLink (USB) or GrayLink (serial) cable.
  3. Launch the connector and click ConnectPick your calculator in the browser's device list. On a NumWorks, open USB mode on the calculator first.
  4. Start craftingDrop files to send them, receive variables, take screenshots or make a backup.

Stuck?

  • Close other calculator software (TI Connect™ CE, TILP) before connecting.
  • On Windows, the connector tells you which driver to install if the browser can't open the calculator: TI Connect™ CE for the TI-84 Plus family and TI-89 Titanium, or WinUSB through Zadig for the rest.
  • On Linux, the TI-83/84 Evo needs your user in the dialout group.
